WHEREAS it is expedient to make provision for the imposition of a corporate income tax on certain Bermuda businesses forming part of large multi-national groups to maintain the attractiveness and competitiveness of Bermuda as a leading financial centre;

Be it enacted by The King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the House of Assembly of Bermuda, and by the authority of the same, as follows:
